How a mission works

What is the MTC?

The Missionary Training Center. Missionaries learning a new language spend their first weeks there studying the language and practising how to teach. Trey started at the training center in Mexico City — the CCM, short for Centro de Capacitación Misional — before heading out to his mission in California.

What is a transfer?

Roughly every six weeks the mission president reassigns missionaries. Trey may stay put or be moved to a new town, and he may get a new companion either way. Transfer news usually shows up in the weekly email, and the Where He Is page gets updated when it does.

What is a companion?

Missionaries are always paired up. Companions live, study, and teach together for the length of a transfer or longer. You will see a lot of names come and go over the course of a mission.

What is a P-day?

Preparation day — one day a week for laundry, groceries, sightseeing, and writing home. That is when the weekly email arrives, and when he is allowed to call or video chat with his family.
